# Frequently Asked Questions

## How does a transaction work?

You tell the agent what you want to buy or sell (crypto or a gift card), the
agent tells you exactly where to send your payment or asset (and where
you'll receive your side of the trade), you confirm the details, and the
agent logs it as a pending request. Our team reviews and confirms it, then
you'll get a WhatsApp message once it's confirmed and again once it's completed.

## What happens after I send my payment or gift card code?

Send a photo of your payment confirmation or the gift card (front and back,
code visible) directly in this chat. It's automatically forwarded to our
team along with your request.

## How do I check on a pending request?

Just ask, mention your lead ID (e.g. "LD12") if you have it, or the agent
can look it up by your phone number.

## What if my rate isn't listed yet?

Ask anyway, the agent will flag it and our team will confirm the exact rate
for your request before anything is finalized.
