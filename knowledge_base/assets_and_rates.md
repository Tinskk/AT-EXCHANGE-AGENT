## How rates work

Rates for each asset are set by the team and can change at any time —
always check with get_rate rather than quoting a remembered number. Gift
card rates are usually a percentage of face value; crypto rates are a price
per unit in local currency; Zelle/PayPal rates reflect processing risk and
fees.
